The Internal Softball Tournament of the University of Antioquia is a sporting event for students who enjoy softball. It is a place to play, learn, meet other students and work as a team. The tournament is also important because it connects training with real games.

This year, three teams registered: Arañita, Smirnoff and Águilas. The tournament system has two rounds, where all the teams play against each other. At the end, the first and second teams play in the final to decide the winner.

According to the poster, the games were scheduled for Thursdays at 2:00 p.m., on October 22 and 29, and November 5, 12, 19 and 26. The games and the final take place at the university's synthetic field.

The tournament brings together players from the university's representative and development softball teams. It is also open to any student who is interested in playing. This means that students with different levels of experience can take part, practise and enjoy the sport.

During a game, players hit the ball, run between bases, catch the ball and try to score points. They need to communicate, help their teammates and make decisions during the game. Other students can watch and support the teams.

THE COACH'S OPINION: RICHARD STEPHENS

RS

Richard Stephens

Softball Coach & Tournament Organizer • UdeA

Coach Richard Stephens explains that the internal tournament is an important part of the students' sports training. It gives players from the development groups the chance to play real games and use the skills they learn in practice. They can also learn to make decisions, control their emotions and work better as a team. The tournament helps players prepare for the university's representative teams and for future university competitions.

COMPARISON WITH SOFTBALL TOURNAMENTS IN MEDELLÍN

The university tournament is smaller than some softball tournaments in Medellín because only three teams registered this year. City tournaments can bring together players from different clubs and communities, while this tournament is focused on the university community.

The university tournament is not as large as some city tournaments, but it is as important for the students who take part. It gives them a chance to learn, practise and play with other university students. In this way, the university event is more focused on learning and preparing players for other competitions.

Both types of tournaments are important because they support sport and teamwork. For many students, one of the best parts of the university tournament is the chance to improve and represent their university.

WHY DID WE CHOOSE THIS EVENT?

We chose this event because softball is an interesting sport and the tournament brings students together. We wanted to show that sport is not only about winning. It is also about learning, making friends and working as a team.

We think this is one of the most valuable activities at the university because students can improve their skills and prepare for bigger challenges. The festival creates a good space for sport, friendship and personal growth.
