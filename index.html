The University of Antioquia organizes a softball festival for students who enjoy sports and teamwork. This event is a good opportunity to play, have fun and meet new people. The tournament takes place at the university's synthetic sports field.

This year, three teams are participating in the festival: Arañita, Smirnoff and Águilas. The tournament has six games. The teams play against each other two times. At the end, the first and second teams play in the final to decide the winner.

The games take place on Thursdays at 2:00 p.m. The dates are October 22 and 29, and November 5, 12, 19 and 26. All the games are played at the university's synthetic field.

One important thing about this festival is that it is open to different students. Some players belong to the university's representative and development softball teams. However, any student who is interested in playing can also participate. This gives new players the opportunity to learn and enjoy this sport.

During the games, players hit the ball, run between bases, catch the ball and work together to score points. They also support their teammates and try to win every game. Other students can watch the matches and support their favorite teams.

COMPARISON WITH SOFTBALL IN MEDELLÍN

This university tournament is a little different from softball tournaments in Medellín. In the city, there are teams from different clubs and communities. These tournaments can have more teams and different players.

In comparison, the university festival has only three teams this year. It is smaller, and its main goal is to bring university students together. Also, students with different levels of experience can play.

Both tournaments are important because they support sports and teamwork. However, the university festival is special because it gives students the opportunity to play, learn and represent their university.

WHY DID WE CHOOSE THIS EVENT?

We chose this event because we think softball is an interesting and fun sport. We also wanted to show the importance of sports at the university.

We like this festival because it brings students together. It is not only about winning games. It is also about making friends, learning new skills and working as a team.

We believe more students should participate in these activities because sports are good for our health and help us have a better university experience.
